More on the Stage

How it works

From blank page to the Stage in three steps.

Pick a beat

Twelve beats, four per scene, each with the rule behind it: how many syllables fit, where the stress falls.

Write with helpers

Put your cursor on a word: rhymes appear. Need an image? Similes, metaphors and theme palettes are in the same panel.

Share and get reviewed

Publish to the Stage. Others upvote, review with banana ratings, promote you to the Spotlight, or remix you.

More than a notebook

Upvotes that save

Every song you upvote lands in your Bunch, so you never lose a favourite.

Perform mode

The beat plays, your lyrics light up word by word, one line per bar. Rehearse anywhere.

Rhyme map

See your rhyme scheme (A, B, A, B) and every rhyming word colour-coded.

Real reviews

Three ratings made for each scene (flow, imagery, hook…) plus a favourite line.

Spotlight

Three promotions a week to push someone else's song onto the front page.

Remix

Start from someone's song with full credit, and make it yours.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Writing, sharing, upvoting and reviewing are free. There are no ads.

Do you use AI to write?

No. Rhymes and syllables come from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, and the similes, metaphors and theme words are a hand-written word bank. Your words stay yours.

Who owns my lyrics?

You do. Sharing a song lets others read, upvote, review and remix it on Yabanana (remixes always credit you). You can unpublish or delete it any time.

Can I keep songs private?

Yes. Songs start as drafts that only you can see. You choose when to share them to the Stage.

What are bananas?

Upvotes. Collect enough and your writer level ripens: Green, Ripening, Ripe, Sweet, Golden Bunch.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any modern browser. Turn your sound on for the beats.
